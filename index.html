About College

Nri Institute Of Technology, Guntur was founded in 2008. It is a private institute and is located in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. Nri Institute Of Technology, Guntur courses are offered to students at the UG, PG and After 10th levels. The institute is approved by AICTE and offers courses in Full Time mode. Nri Institute Of Technology, Guntur offers a total of 20 courses in streams such as Engineering, IT and Software and Business and Management Studies. Some of the popular courses offered at the institute are B.E. / B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, MBA/PGDM, MCA and After 10th Diploma and the total seat intake for the available courses is 2070. These courses are offered to students across several specialisations such as Civil Engineering, Computer Science Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Mechanical Engineering. Further, the fee for all these courses ranges between INR 70,000 to 1,60,000. Apart from this, the Nri Institute Of Technology, Guntur campus has a state-of-the-art infrastructure with all the advanced facilities.
